# Figure captions (factual; one line per figure)

Every figure is saved as PDF and PNG at half-page width in `results/figures/` without an in-figure title; the plotted values are in the CSV named in the second column. Colours, line styles, markers and hatches are fixed per model across all figures (colour-blind-safe Okabe–Ito palette); setting A is the same model as the Part I LSTM and shares its colour.
